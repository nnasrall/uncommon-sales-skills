# Output Templates: QBR Prep

## The QBR pack (produce this)

```
# QBR Prep: [Account] - [quarter]
Original goals they bought for: [the problem statement + success criteria]

## Value delivered (tied to their goals)
| Win this quarter | The goal it maps to | In their metric |
|------------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| [win] | [original goal / success criterion] | [number] |

## Risk assessment
| Risk | Evidence (behavior) | The plan |
|------|---------------------|----------|
| [low engagement / missed goal / leadership change / competitor] | [what you actually see] | [your move] |

## Expansion openings (ranked)
| Opening | Type | Who to engage | Impact / ease |
|---------|------|---------------|---------------|
| [use case / team / problem] | new use case / adjacent team / usage signal | [name] | [H/M/L] |

## QBR agenda
1. Their success this quarter [lead here, in their metrics]
2. Goals on track / at risk [proactive, with a plan for each risk]
3. What is next [expansion framed as their next outcome]
4. Open discussion + next steps

## Open gaps
- [MISSING: ...] e.g. original success criteria not documented, usage data unavailable
```

## Reminders

- Build the value section backward from the goals they bought for.
- Bring every risk with a plan attached. Name it before they do.
- Frame expansion as their next outcome, not your upsell.
- Lead with them. The moment it reads as a pitch, you are a vendor, not a partner.
- No em dashes, no jargon.
