# Skill Reference

All ten skills, grouped by deal stage. Each entry: what it does, when it fires, the framework it runs, and the deliverable it hands you.

---

## Prospecting and account entry

### multithread
**Does:** Maps the real buying committee (not the org chart) and drafts the outreach to widen coverage. Works from a new account, or an existing live deal riding on a single thread.
**Fires on:** "multithread this account," "who else should I be talking to," "this deal is single-threaded," "widen this deal."
**Framework:** Buying-group roles plus SLIP (Security, Legal, IT, Procurement), the shadow org map, the "which means" chain, and the exec-level multithreading email series.
**Deliverable:** A committee map, a value hypothesis per stakeholder, drafted outreach for each, and a sequence.

### account-planning
**Does:** Builds a point of view and a strategic account plan for a target account, or finds and ranks expansion whitespace in an existing one.
**Fires on:** "build an account plan," "give me a POV on this account," "find expansion," "where can we grow this account."
**Framework:** POV built on what is changing, the strategic vision, the compelling event, and the opportunity map ranked by executive priority.
**Deliverable:** A strategic account plan plus a first outreach message.

## Discovery

### call-prep
**Does:** Turns "I have a call" into a Meeting Brief you walk in with.
**Fires on:** "prep me for this call," "build an agenda," "what should I ask on discovery," "brief me before this demo."
**Framework:** The Problem/Process/Payoff discovery roadmap, a POV and four-sentence soundbite, the opening frame, and the Commitment Ladder.
**Deliverable:** A Meeting Brief: objective, POV, attendees and their questions, opening frame, planned discovery, threats, and the ask.

### call-follow-up
**Does:** Turns a call or group demo into the follow-up that moves the deal.
**Fires on:** "follow up after my call," "debrief this call," "who was on the demo and what do I send each of them."
**Framework:** Stakeholder mapping, the five-element problem statement in the buyer's words, the two forwardable-email patterns, and the discovery lookback.
**Deliverable:** A stakeholder map, the confirmed problem, forwardable per-person follow-ups, a champion debrief, and a self-debrief on where discovery was thin.

## Building consensus

### champion-enablement
**Does:** Builds the internal-selling kit your champion uses in the meetings you are not in.
**Fires on:** "help me arm my champion," "build a one-pager my champion can share," "how do I handle the skeptic," "objection responses."
**Framework:** The three I's, the influence map and messaging matrix, the four-sentence soundbite, Acknowledge-Diagnose-Redirect with the urgency equation, and the consulting-style exec summary.
**Deliverable:** A champion kit: the three I's read, an internal meeting map, a soundbite, objection reframes, a skeptic plan, and a forwardable one-pager.

## Demo and evaluation

### competitive-displacement
**Does:** Builds the narrative and plan to unseat an incumbent without a feature war.
**Fires on:** "we're up against [competitor]," "displace the incumbent," "how do I unseat," "they're already using [tool]."
**Framework:** Sell the approach before the product, be different not better, name a villain, and trap-setting questions.
**Deliverable:** A displacement plan: the incumbent read, two to three differences in approach and outcome, a switching story, and trap-setting questions.

## Business case and mid-funnel

### one-page-business-case
**Does:** Builds a customer-specific one-page business case from your deal data, scores it, and prescribes the next move to strengthen it.
**Fires on:** "build a business case," "write the business case for [account]," "turn this deal into a business case."
**Framework:** The one-page business case (headline, problem, approach, target outcomes, required investment), scored against a five-section rubric.
**Deliverable:** The drafted case, a scored readiness rubric, prescribed next actions, and an optional formatted export.

### deal-advancement
**Does:** Diagnoses why a deal stalled and generates moves to restart it, or builds a mutual action plan to close it.
**Fires on:** "why is this deal stuck," "the deal went quiet," "build a mutual action plan," "write a changed-direction email."
**Framework:** Math-problem-versus-drama, deal momentum, the change equation, and the mutual action plan as a training plan backdated from a compelling event.
**Deliverable:** A stuck-deal diagnosis with re-engagement moves, or a backdated mutual action plan with owners and dates.

## Account management

### qbr-prep
**Does:** Preps a customer quarterly review that leads with their success, gets ahead of risk, and opens expansion.
**Fires on:** "prep for my QBR," "quarterly business review," "renewal review," "customer review prep."
**Framework:** Value tied to the goals they bought for, risk signals, expansion whitespace, and the 60-Second Deal Review.
**Deliverable:** A QBR pack: value delivered, a risk assessment with plans, ranked expansion openings, and an agenda.

---

## Managing up

### manage-up
**Does:** Preps an AE for a forecast call, pipeline review, or deal review with leadership, and turns each risk into a specific ask.
**Fires on:** "prep me for my forecast call," "deal review with leadership," "what should I flag to my boss," "I need an intro or budget from my VP."
**Framework:** Managing up (enable leadership like a champion), forecasting on group behavior not champion optimism, the 60-Second Deal Review, math-versus-drama, and the specific ask delivered as a drafted asset.
**Deliverable:** A leadership review: a graded forecast, 60-second deal reviews, the big bets, and an ask list with each ask drafted and ready to send.

---

## A note on overlap

Skills cross-reference each other. `call-follow-up` hands a real blocker to `champion-enablement`. `champion-enablement` points to `one-page-business-case` for the full scored version. `qbr-prep` (customer-facing) is the sibling of `manage-up` (internal-facing). Use whichever matches the moment you are in.
